# Funnel KPI report

Data: 9,240 leads, 3,561 converted. Overall conversion 38.5% (95% CI 37.5%-39.5%).

## What stands out

1. **Engagement (Total Time Spent on Website):** the biggest jump is at 600. Leads above 600 convert at 68% (n=2,952) vs 15% (n=4,095) for leads with some activity up to 600. Use this threshold to prioritise follow-up.
2. **Lead source: welingak website** converts at 99% (95% CI 95%-100%, n=142) vs 39% overall, which is 2.6x the average.
3. **Lead origin: lead add form** converts at 92% (95% CI 90%-94%, n=718) vs 39% overall, which is 2.4x the average.
4. **Lead source: reference** converts at 92% (95% CI 89%-94%, n=534) vs 39% overall, which is 2.4x the average.
5. **Last activity: email bounced** converts at only 8% (95% CI 6%-11%, n=326) vs 39% overall.
6. **Last activity: olark chat conversation** converts at only 9% (95% CI 7%-11%, n=973) vs 39% overall.
7. **Last activity: converted to lead** converts at only 13% (95% CI 10%-16%, n=428) vs 39% overall.
8. **Last activity: sms sent** converts at 63% (95% CI 61%-65%, n=2745) vs 39% overall, which is 1.6x the average.

## Caveats

- Public X Education lead-scoring data (9,240 leads, no dates), so no weekly trend can be shown.
- Last activity can be recorded after conversion (for example a welcome SMS), so it is a symptom as much as a driver.
- Leads with 0 seconds on site include leads added manually or through offline channels.
- Groups with fewer than 100 leads are merged into 'other (small groups)' and not interpreted.
- Associations only: a high rate does not prove the channel or activity caused the conversion.
