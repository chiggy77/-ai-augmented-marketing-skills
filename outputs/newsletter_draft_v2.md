Subject: Time on site separates leads more than channel does
We analysed 8,466 inbound leads from a public lead-scoring dataset to see where conversion comes from. The clearest signal is not in the channel report we usually read.

**Time on site.** Leads who spent more than 10 minutes on the website converted at 67%. Leads who spent up to 10 minutes converted at 15%. Ranking leads by time on site is the simplest way to decide who gets a call first. Send the first group to sales the same day. Leave the second group to automated nurturing.

**Channels.** Google converts at 40% and organic search at 38%. Direct traffic converts at 32%. Chat leads convert at only 25%, so we should improve chat follow-up before we spend more on it.

**Email hygiene.** Leads whose email bounced converted at 5%. Leads who asked not to be emailed converted at 12%. Cleaning the list is a quick win.

These are associations in one public dataset, not proof of cause. Please check them against our live data before changing any budget.
