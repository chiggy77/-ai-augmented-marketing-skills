# Funnel KPI report

Data: 8,466 leads, 2,883 converted. Overall conversion 34.1% (95% CI 33.1%-35.1%).

## What stands out

1. **Engagement (Total Time Spent on Website):** the biggest jump is at 600. Leads above 600 convert at 67% (n=2,825) vs 15% (n=4,035) for leads with some activity up to 600. Use this threshold to prioritise follow-up.
2. **Last activity: email bounced** converts at only 5% (95% CI 3%-8%, n=311) vs 34% overall.
3. **Last activity: olark chat conversation** converts at only 8% (95% CI 7%-10%, n=968) vs 34% overall.
4. **Last activity: sms sent** converts at 59% (95% CI 57%-61%, n=2401) vs 34% overall, which is 1.7x the average.
5. **Do not email flag: yes** converts at only 12% (95% CI 9%-14%, n=689) vs 34% overall.
6. **Last activity: converted to lead** converts at only 13% (95% CI 10%-16%, n=428) vs 34% overall.
7. **Last activity: email link clicked** converts at only 20% (95% CI 16%-26%, n=244) vs 34% overall.
8. **Last activity: page visited on website** converts at only 21% (95% CI 18%-25%, n=621) vs 34% overall.

## Caveats

- Digital inbound leads only (Landing Page Submission and API); manually added and imported leads are excluded.
- Public X Education lead-scoring data (9,240 leads in total, no dates), so no weekly trend can be shown.
- Last activity can be recorded after conversion (for example a welcome SMS), so it is a symptom as much as a driver.
- Groups with fewer than 100 leads are merged into 'other (small groups)' and not interpreted.
- Associations only: a high rate does not prove the channel or activity caused the conversion.
