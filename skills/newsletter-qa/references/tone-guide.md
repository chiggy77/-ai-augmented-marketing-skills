# Tone guide (example, adapt to the brand)

- Plain, confident, specific. Short sentences (under 28 words), active verbs.
- Say what we found, what it means and what to do next, in that order.
- Prefer numbers with their context ("67% vs 15%") to adjectives ("huge").
- Avoid buzzwords (game-changer, leverage, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge).
- Admit limits in one sentence. Never overclaim cause.
- One call to action, written as a verb phrase ("Open the full report").
